UniMind.ai Privacy Policy

Version: 2.0 | Effective date: June 3, 2026

Operator: Beijing Rongzhi Gaoke (北京融知高科), referred to below as “we”.

1. Introduction and Scope

1.1 Beijing Rongzhi Gaoke values users’ privacy. This Policy explains how we collect, use, store, share, transfer, and protect personal information, and the rights you have.

1.2 This Policy applies throughout your use of our services through UniMind.ai, including its website, mobile applications, and other access methods we provide.

1.3 Read and understand this Policy before using the services. Contact us using the channels in Section 14 if you have questions.

1.4 Important notice: provisions materially affecting your rights are highlighted in bold. Please read them carefully.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following information when you use our services.

2.1 Information You Provide

  • Registration: email address, display name, and password (stored encrypted). Email verification codes verify your identity during registration;
  • Profile: optional information such as your avatar and biography;
  • Institution: for institutional accounts, the institution name, your role (student, teacher, or administrator), and invitation code may be required;
  • Diagnostics: answers and feedback submitted during diagnostic assessments;
  • Payments: third-party providers (Alipay, WeChat Pay, Stripe, Airwallex) process payments. We receive payment results and order status, and do not store complete card numbers, bank-card passwords, or similar sensitive payment information.

2.2 Information Generated Through Use

  • Learning data: answers, accuracy, study time, course progress, plans, diagnostic results, and knowledge-graph mastery scores;
  • AI interactions: conversations with Agents (including XiaoYu and the workbench), AI replies, and history;
  • AI memory: profiles, learning-preference analyses, and semantic memory produced from interactions using mem0;
  • Question generation: generated questions, review records, and revision history;
  • Courses: uploaded course materials, watch history, and note content.

2.3 Automatically Collected Information

  • Device: browser type and version, operating system, device type, screen resolution, and language settings;
  • Network: IP address, network provider, access times, and referring page;
  • Logs: visited URLs, clicks, time spent, and feature-usage frequency;
  • Cookies and similar technologies: see Section 5.

2.4 Information We Do Not Actively Collect

Unless you provide it voluntarily or law requires it, we do not actively collect:

  • Identity-card, passport, or other identity-document numbers;
  • Precise GPS location;
  • Contacts, text messages, or call records;
  • Facial features, fingerprints, or other biometric information.

3. How We Use Information

We use collected information for the following purposes.

3.1 Providing and Maintaining Services

  • Create and manage accounts and verify identity;
  • Provide core functions such as AI coaching, question generation, courses, and diagnostic assessments;
  • Maintain knowledge graphs, progress tracking, mastery analysis, and other learning-management functions;
  • Process payments and orders and manage paid services and subscriptions.

3.2 Personalization and Intelligent Functions

  • Generate personalized recommendations and plans from learning data;
  • Provide continuous, personalized Agent conversations using interaction history;
  • Build and update mastery profiles and improve diagnostic accuracy;
  • Use the Memorix memory-scheduling algorithm to adjust review plans dynamically from learning history.

3.3 Security and Operations

  • Protect accounts and prevent fraud, abuse, and unauthorized access;
  • Monitor system operation, troubleshoot, and improve performance;
  • Analyze data and statistics to improve functions and user experience;
  • Monitor AI-service health through an AI circuit breaker to maintain service quality.

3.4 Compliance and Communication

  • Comply with laws, regulations, and regulatory requirements;
  • Send service notices, security alerts, and policy updates;
  • Respond to inquiries, feedback, and complaints.

3.5 De-identification and Research

After de-identification or anonymization, we may use data for:

  • AI-model training and optimization, using de-identified data only;
  • Statistical analysis for educational research and product improvement;
  • Aggregate reports that do not identify individuals.

4. Automated AI Decisions and Profiles

4.1 Automated decisions: some functions analyze learning data using AI and automatically generate decision suggestions, including:

  • Learning paths: recommendations for content and sequence based on mastery and learning history;
  • Adaptive difficulty: adjusting question difficulty from answer performance;
  • Diagnostic assessments: analyzing weak areas from test results;
  • Profiles: deriving learning preferences and ability profiles from interactions.

4.2 Human intervention: you may request human review of automated results. If you dispute an AI suggestion or assessment, request review using the contact channels below.

4.3 Right to refuse: you may refuse decisions based solely on automated processing that materially affect your rights, although some personalized functions may then be unavailable.

4.4 Transparency: we strive to make automated decisions explainable. Agents will try to explain the reasoning behind their suggestions.

5. Cookies and Similar Technologies

5.1 We use cookies and similar technologies for secure operation and user experience, including:

  • Necessary cookies: maintain sign-in (httpOnly cookies), session security, and CSRF protection. These are necessary for normal service operation and cannot be opted out of;
  • Functional cookies: remember preferences such as theme, language, and layout;
  • Analytics cookies: understand use and monitor performance to improve services.

5.2 Manage cookie preferences in your browser. Disabling necessary cookies may prevent some functions from working normally.

5.3 We do not use cookies for cross-site tracking or provide browsing data to third-party advertisers.

6. Storage and Security

6.1 Storage Location

Personal information is stored on servers in the People’s Republic of China. Our cloud-service providers follow industry-standard security practices.

6.2 Retention

We retain personal information only as long as necessary for the purposes in this Policy. Specific periods are:

  • Account information: during the account’s lifetime and for 30 days after closure, to address possible disputes and compliance requirements;
  • Learning data: during the account’s lifetime, then deleted or anonymized after closure;
  • AI conversations: during the account’s lifetime. Specific history may be deleted at any time using Reset Conversation;
  • AI memory: during the account’s lifetime. Specific memory entries may be viewed and deleted using Memory Management;
  • Payments and orders: 5 years after transaction completion, as required by the E-Commerce Law;
  • Logs: 6 months after creation, as required by the Cybersecurity Law;
  • De-identified statistics: may be retained indefinitely for improvement and statistical analysis.

6.3 Security Measures

We use industry-standard measures, including:

  • Encryption in transit throughout the site using HTTPS/TLS;
  • Password protection using irreversible, one-way hashing;
  • AES symmetric encryption for sensitive fields;
  • Role-based access control and least privilege;
  • AI-service circuit breakers to limit the spread of abnormal requests;
  • Regular security audits and vulnerability scans;
  • Operational logs and security audit trails.

6.4 Despite reasonable measures, technical limitations and malicious activity mean that absolute internet security is impossible. We will notify you promptly of personal-information security incidents as required by law.

7. Sharing and Disclosure

We do not share, transfer, or publicly disclose personal information to third parties except in the following circumstances.

7.1 Your Consent

We may share personal information with third parties after obtaining your explicit consent.

7.2 Third-Party Providers

To provide services, we may share necessary information with these categories of providers:

  • Payments: Alipay, WeChat Pay, Stripe, and Airwallex process transactions using order and amount information;
  • Cloud storage: Alibaba Cloud OSS stores videos and files using file metadata;
  • AI models: DeepSeek and other model APIs process AI requests using conversation content;
  • Email: Resend sends verification messages and notifications using email addresses;
  • Cloud infrastructure: server and database hosting providers.

We provide only the minimum information necessary for the service purpose and impose contractual information-protection obligations.

7.3 Legal Requirements

We may disclose personal information to comply with laws, legal proceedings, or mandatory requirements of competent government authorities.

7.4 Protecting Rights

We may share information within a reasonably necessary scope to protect the lawful rights of Beijing Rongzhi Gaoke, you, or other users, including fraud prevention and security.

7.5 Institutional Sharing

If you use an institutional account, its administrators may view some learning data, such as answers, progress, and diagnostic results, to provide teaching guidance. You are informed of this sharing relationship during registration.

7.6 Anonymous Data

De-identified or anonymized data that cannot identify a specific person and cannot be restored is not personal information. We may use it for improvement, academic research, or statistical reports for third parties.

8. Your Rights

Under Chinese laws and regulations, you have the following rights. Exercise them through settings or by contacting us.

8.1 Access

You may access account information, learning data, AI memory, and other personal information. View basic information under Settings → Personal Information and AI memory under AI Memory Management.

8.2 Correction

You may request correction of inaccurate or outdated information. Change supported fields in Settings or contact us for assistance.

8.3 Deletion

You may request deletion when:

  • The purpose has been achieved or the information is no longer necessary;
  • You withdraw consent and no other legal basis permits continued processing;
  • Information has been processed unlawfully;
  • Other circumstances provided by law apply.

8.4 Portability

You may request export of personal information. Settings → Data Export supports standard-format exports of learning data and personal information.

8.5 Withdrawing Consent

For consent-based processing, you may withdraw consent at any time. This does not affect the lawfulness of prior processing. You may:

  • Change browser cookie settings;
  • Disable personalized recommendations;
  • Contact us to withdraw other consent.

8.6 Account Closure

You may apply through Settings → Account Security → Close Account. After closure:

  • Personal information will be deleted or anonymized within 30 days;
  • Published anonymized statistics will remain;
  • Payment and order records will remain for legally required periods;
  • Closure cannot be undone.

We verify your identity before closure for security. The same account cannot register again within 30 days after closure.

8.7 Response Times

We will respond within 15 working days after receiving a request. Complex requests may take longer, up to 30 working days, and we will explain why.

9.1 Personal information is primarily stored within the People’s Republic of China.

9.2 Using overseas third-party services, such as AI APIs or international payment gateways, may transfer some data, including conversations or payment information, overseas. In those circumstances, we will:

11.2 Administrators must comply with the institutional service agreement and applicable laws when accessing or using data. They may not use personal data for purposes unrelated to teaching management.

11.3 If you object to your institution’s data access, tell us. We will help investigate and address it.
